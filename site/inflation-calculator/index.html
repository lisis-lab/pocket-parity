How we calculate

A = amount · Iyear = consumer price index for that year

  • Value: A × Ito / Ifrom
  • Cumulative inflation: Ito / Ifrom − 1
  • Average per year: (Ito / Ifrom)1/years − 1

The index is the World Bank's annual CPI series for the United States; the most recent years come from the BLS CPI-U annual average, chained to the World Bank's last published year.
