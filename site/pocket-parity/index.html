How we calculate

A = amount you take · E = exchange rate (destination currency per 1 home unit) · P = price level (US = 100) · R = Pdest / Phome

  • At the exchange rate: A × E
  • It will feel like: A / R
  • To spend like at home: A × R (A × R × E in local cash)
  • Each item: price × E × Pdest,category / Phome,category

Price levels come from the World Bank's International Comparison Program (2021 benchmark, household consumption without housing for the overall figure). Rates are the ECB's daily reference rates. Figures are national averages: Zurich costs more than rural Switzerland.
