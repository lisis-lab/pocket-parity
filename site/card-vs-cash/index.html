How we calculate

A = amount in the local currency · E = mid-market rate (local currency per 1 unit of yours) · fees as decimals (3% = 0.03)

  • Reference: A / E
  • Card: A/E × (1 + markup) × (1 + foreign fee)
  • ATM: A/E × (1 + markup) + bank fee + ATM owner's fee / E
  • Exchange bureau: A/E × (1 + markup)

The mid-market rate is the ECB daily reference rate; you can type your own. Fees vary by bank and card, so the starting values are only examples.
