How we calculate

hc = what you spend per day at home in category c · E = exchange rate · Rc = Pdestination,c / Phome,c · D = days

  • There, per category: hc × E × Rc
  • Trip total: D × Σ hc × E × Rc
  • Versus home: (Σ hc × Rc) / Σ hc

Price levels come from the World Bank International Comparison Program (ICP), 2021 edition. The exchange rate is the ECB daily reference rate. Starting amounts are a USD 150 / 20 / 25 / 30 / 15 day adjusted to your home price level; change them to match how you travel.
